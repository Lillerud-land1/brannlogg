Oppdatering og kjelder

  • Brannstatistikk (brannstatistikk.no, DSB): hovudkjelda. Her registrerer Stord brann og redning alle oppdraga sine, også små, men utan stad og detaljar. Automatiske brannalarmar og avbrotne utrykkingar er berre med under «Alt».
  • Politiloggen (Politiet, NLOD 2.0) gir stad og kva som skjedde, og kjem ofte først: alle brannar i Stord kommune, og andre hendingar der politiet nemner brannvesenet eller nødetatane, som trafikkulykker, ulykker på sjøen og utslepp.
  • Nyheitssaker frå mellom anna Radio Haugaland, Sunnhordland, Stord24, NRK og Haugesunds Avis blir funne automatisk og lenka til hendingar frå Politiloggen og brannstatistikken. Nyheiter blir aldri eigne hendingar. Ein sjeldan gong kan ei lenke vere feil.
  • Skogbrannfare: rekna ut med Fire Weather Index-metoden frå vêrdata frå Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Offisielle farevarsel frå Meteorologisk institutt (CC BY 4.0).
  • Kart: kystlinje © OpenStreetMap-bidragsytarar (ODbL), kommunegrense og stadnamn frå Kartverket.

Appen viser berre det politiet har loggført. Oppdrag som berre brannvesenet handterer, som katt i tre, vasslekkasjar og automatiske brannalarmar, kjem sjeldan med. Står det «Nødetatane», har politiet ikkje nemnt brannvesenet direkte. Alvorsgraden er rekna ut frå teksten i loggen og er ikkje ei offisiell vurdering.

In short: Brannlogg Stord has no accounts, no cookies, no tracking or user statistics, and no ads.

Who runs it

Brannlogg Stord is a free, private website made by Leander Wågen Lillerud. It is not an official service of the police, the fire service or Stord municipality. Contact: brannloggstord@gmail.com.

What is stored on your device

Your settings, the time of the newest incident you have seen (for the “NEW” label), whether you have closed the privacy notice, and a copy of the site so it can open offline. This stays in your browser and is never sent to me. See Cookies.

Technical information shared with others

  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) hosts the website. As with any website, they receive your IP address and browser information when the page loads, for operation and security.
  • Google Fonts: The fonts are loaded from Google, which then receives your IP address.

I do not have access to this information. See the privacy policies of GitHub and Google.

Information about incidents

The site shows public information from Politiloggen (Norwegian Police, NLOD 2.0) and brannstatistikk.no (DSB), weather data from MET Norway and Open-Meteo, and links to news stories. Police texts and news headlines may mention places and incidents. If you think something on the site concerns you and should be corrected or removed, please get in touch.

Phone notifications

Notifications are sent through the ntfy app (ntfy.sh). I do not know who subscribes. See ntfy's privacy policy.

Email

If you email a bug report or idea, it is only used to reply to you and improve the site. Emails are stored in Gmail (Google) and deleted when the matter is closed.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of information about you. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (datatilsynet.no).

Changes

This policy may change. The date at the top shows the latest change.

Brannlogg Stord does not use cookies, and no tracking or analytics tools either.

Local storage in your browser

So that the features you choose can work, the site stores a little information in your browser. It is never sent anywhere.

  • brannlogg_innst: your settings (language, colours, appearance, text size, motion and what the app opens with).
  • brannlogg_sist_sett: the time of the newest incident you have seen, so new incidents can get the “NEW” label.
  • brannlogg_personvern_ok: that you have closed the privacy notice.
  • Saved copy (service worker): a copy of the site so it can open offline.

Other services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and is hosted on GitHub Pages. They may receive technical information such as your IP address. See Privacy.

How to delete it

You can reset your settings under Settings → Reset. Everything else is deleted by clearing site data for bls.lillerud.com in your browser settings.
